**Welcome to the devkitPro wiki**

This wiki is intended to become a documentation repository for the devkitPro toolchains and their supporting libraries. Right now it's very much a work in progress but we hope to expand into a useful resource for budding homebrew programmers and veterans of the homebrew scene alike.

Users are welcome to add to and expand these pages but should be warned that the devkitPro staff take a very firm stance on piracy. Any links to sites dealing with copyright infringement will be deleted and the user's access to the wiki removed.
